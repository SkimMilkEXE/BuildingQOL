WorldEdit-style building tools for Terraria: select an area, copy, flip and paste it, save it as a schematic, and reshape it with chat commands. Everything can be undone.

Features:
- Selection with a live outline, corner markers and size label
- Copy and paste tiles, walls, slopes, paint, chest names, sign text and tile entities (chest items are left out so nothing can be duplicated)
- See-through paste preview of the actual blocks and walls
- Flip the clipboard horizontally or vertically, furniture stays in one piece
- Named schematics you can load into any world
- Undo and redo, up to 50 actions back
- Shape and fill commands: /fill, /outline, /hollow, /blockswap, /floodfill, /drain, /clear
- Terrain Wand for raising, lowering, smoothing and roughening ground
- Optional tile grid and cursor highlight
- Multiplayer sync

Default keybinds (rebindable in Settings > Controls):
[ / ] - Set selection corner 1 / corner 2
Backspace - Clear selection
Delete - Erase tiles and walls in selection
C - Copy selection
V - Paste at cursor
X - Flip clipboard horizontally
Z / Y - Undo / Redo
S / L - Save / load the default schematic slot
G - Toggle grid overlay
H - Toggle cursor highlight
M - Cycle Terrain Wand mode

Commands (type in chat, /qolhelp lists them in-game):
/fill <block> - Fill the selection with a tile, wall or liquid
/outline <block> - Place a 1-tile border around the edge of the selection
/hollow - Remove the blocks inside the selection, keeping a 1-tile shell
/blockswap <from> <to> - Replace one tile, wall or liquid with another
/floodfill <water|lava|honey|shimmer> - Fill the open space with liquid and let it settle
/drain - Remove all liquid in the selection
/clear - Erase tiles, walls and liquid in the selection
/flip <h|v> - Flip the clipboard
/schem save|load|delete <name> - Manage named schematics
/schem list - List saved schematics
/tilename - Show the internal names of what's under your cursor
/qolhelp - List all commands

Terrain Wand: new characters spawn with one, or craft it from 10 Wood at a Work Bench. Left-click to Raise, Lower, Smooth or Roughen a strip of ground centered on your cursor. M cycles the mode.
